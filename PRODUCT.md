# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and technical interviewers evaluating Michael Fernandez for junior or entry-level full-stack engineering roles. Secondary: collaborators who want to inspect a specific build, its live deployment, or source code. Visitors arrive to establish capability quickly, then follow a project or contact path.

## Product Purpose

This is Michael Fernandez's personal portfolio: a concise, credible record of full-stack work, systems thinking, and practical engineering range. Success is a visitor understanding the kind of work he ships and having a clear route to inspect the work, open his résumé, or get in touch.

## Positioning

Inferred from the existing portfolio copy and project evidence: an early-career full-stack engineer who connects type-safe application work with deployment, infrastructure, hardware tuning, and disciplined AI-assisted workflows.

## Operating Context

Recruiters and engineering teams commonly scan the homepage quickly on desktop or mobile, then open a live project, source repository, résumé, GitHub profile, or email client. Project screenshots and deployment links are the concrete evidence; some self-hosted work intentionally remains source-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router site deployed under the `/portfolio` base path.
- Featured projects can carry live links, source links, and optional screenshots; source-only self-hosted projects must not link to private or auth-gated instances.
- The portfolio's factual copy, project links, screenshots, GitHub, email, phone, and résumé route must remain intact.
- This redesign is an inferred direction authorized by the user's request to improve the overall UI/UX; no new testimonials, clients, metrics, or commercial claims may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Michael Fernandez / `haruhadj`; direct, technically literate, candid voice. Existing real project screenshots and GitHub are the primary proof assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portfolio content and destination links: `app/page.tsx`.
- Project screenshots: `public/shots/`.
- Printable résumé and project details: `app/resume/page.tsx`.
- The existing featured Faculty Evaluation System entry is user work in progress and must be preserved.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with real work before self-description.
2. Make technical range legible without inflating claims.
3. Keep routes to evidence and contact immediate.
4. Respect the distinction between public deployments and private self-hosted systems.
5. Let the interface feel intentional, fast, and easy to scan.
